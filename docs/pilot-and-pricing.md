# Pilot plan and pricing options

## Recommended sequence
1. **School pilot first (4 weeks, 1–2 classes, about 30–60 children aged 9–12).** Evidence from a school makes the bank conversation much easier.
2. **Bank pilot second**, using the school results, in a branded edition (e.g. `VITE_EDITION=abc-bank`) for Dubai and Abu Dhabi.

## School pilot design
| Week | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0 | Teacher creates a class in Teacher mode; parents get the class code and consent in-app. Children take the **before** check on the first two stops. |
| 1–3 | Two 20-minute sessions a week in class (or as homework): Money Basics → Salary Street → Family Budget, then one path of the child's choice. Teacher uses the printable worksheets for one lesson a week. |
| 4 | Children take the **after** checks. Short survey for children (fun, difficulty) and parents (behaviour at home: saving, talking about money). |

**Measures to report**
- Learning: average before → after improvement per stop (shown in Teacher mode).
- Engagement: % of children active each week, minutes per week, stops completed, real-life missions confirmed by parents.
- Behaviour signals: Town Bank deposits, savings goals set, parent survey answers.
- Satisfaction: child "fun" rating, teacher and parent ratings, quotes.

**Targets for a strong result:** at least 25% average improvement on checks, at least 70% weekly active, at least 4 out of 5 average fun rating.

## Pricing options (for discussion)
| Customer | Model | Indicative range* |
|---|---|---|
| Bank (white-label) | One-off setup (branding, product names, city facts, compliance review) + annual licence + per active child per year | Setup [AED 40–80k]; licence [AED 60–150k/yr]; [AED 10–25] per active child/yr |
| Bank (sponsorship of the free app) | Annual sponsorship of a city edition, with aggregated impact reporting | [AED 100–300k/yr] per city |
| School | Per student per year, teacher tools included; free for a first class | [AED 20–40] per student/yr |
| Families | Free (sponsored), or a small family subscription without sponsors | [AED 0–15/month] |

*Placeholders to test in discovery interviews. Validate willingness to pay with 5–8 banks' youth/retail heads and 5–10 school heads before fixing prices.

## What partners get
- **Bank:** branded edition (name, colours, product names in the Junior Club stop), parent-only product information, aggregated impact report (learning gains, active families). Positioning: financial-literacy CSR and future-customer trust, not direct sales to children (see `partner-policy.md`).
- **School:** class dashboard, worksheets aligned to financial-literacy topics, certificates with the school's name, before/after evidence for parents and inspectors.

## Before the first pilot
- Legal review of `privacy-and-consent.md` and the partner contract/DPA.
- Fact and Arabic review (`content-review.md`).
- Paid hosting with a disk (`render.yaml`) and a custom domain.
- Optional: recorded narration for ages 7–9, and a professional illustration pass.
